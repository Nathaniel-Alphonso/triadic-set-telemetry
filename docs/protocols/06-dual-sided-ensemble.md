# The Dual-Sided Ensemble — One Candidate, Both Directions (Public Protocol Summary)

Each single-direction statistic reads only one side of the degeneration signature: the collapse side catches a repetition excursion *above* honest traffic; the reworked anchorage side catches a floor honest traffic *dips below*. Either side alone misses what the other sees. The dual-sided ensemble is the pre-registered candidate that reads both: per document, each side is standardized against its leg's clean population, and the document score is the higher of the two — positive means loop-like evidence from either direction.

## 1. The headwind, stated before any measurement

Under the null — a clean document — the higher of two standardized readings sits above zero by construction, and its spread widens. The clean population's combined score therefore sits higher than either side alone, and the separation bar is **strictly harder for the ensemble than for a single side**. This is pinned up front: passing the bar is a stronger claim, and failing it is *not* evidence that the sides are weak — they stand separately in their own records.

## 2. The uncorrupted claim — calibration and test on disjoint populations

The ensemble's per-side thresholds are calibrated on the **banked walks** — the development record, which stands untouched and is never voided. The separation bar and the fire-rate falsifier are then applied **out-of-sample on four genuinely new walk legs**: the same corpora families, fresh tags, the banked instrument untouched. The new legs are the virgin test set for the ensemble alone; in-sample fire rates are what the calibration guarantees by construction, so the falsifier's teeth live entirely on the new walks.

## 3. The budget and the falsifier

A clean fire-rate budget is pinned per leg and split across the armed sides. Thresholds are **calibration artifacts** — empirical quantiles of the calibration population — never adjudicated constants. The falsifier is binding: **any leg's combined clean fire-rate over budget kills the candidate, even if the separation bar passes on every leg.** Small legs carry honest count-based accounting with the statistical upper bound reported alongside, so a rate claim is never overstated where the population cannot resolve it. The binding operating point — the wired threshold and its budget — is deferred to the wiring phase under its own pre-registration; nothing wires here.

## 4. Failure forensics — the standing directive

Every failing or near-bar leg readout carries the interpretive block: **how** it failed (which population, which tail, the mechanism shape), **where** (the leg, document, and step localization the telemetry allows), **why** (the story the numbers tell, honestly separated from the verdict statistic), and **what next** (the educated options). Presented for the owner's stamps — never cherry-picked, never silently skipped.

## 5. No fallback

The candidate table closes at this one ensemble. The combination rule is not one of several tried; there is no additive alternative waiting in reserve. If the ensemble fails, the candidate fails, and the record says so.

## 6. What this sheet does not do

No actuation, no wiring, no threshold setting. The standing statistics remain the statistics of record until the adjudication passes. Parallel investigations (the empty-generation look-in, the code-leg all-fail investigation) ride their own pre-registrations, non-gating.
