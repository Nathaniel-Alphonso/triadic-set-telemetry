# Species Development — the Development-Lane Discipline (Public Protocol Summary)

Before any instrument earns standing on the record lane, it is specified, tested, and either grounded or killed on the development lane. This document is the protocol record of that lane: the pre-registration discipline under which candidate instruments ("species") and diagnostic probes are drafted, pinned, run, and closed — development data only, never a run of record, and the record lane's acceptance bar never computed on development bytes.

## 1. The budget law — the mechanism is the product

The development lane exists to exhaust every candidate mechanic on development bytes before the record lane's GPU is paid for. A development-lane number is never the product; the mechanism, specified to maximal precision, is. The lane's deliverable is a prepared record lane — never a banked result.

## 2. Paper before build — the pinning stamp

Every sheet is drafted on paper before any build, before any read, and presented for the owner's pinning stamp. At the stamp the bytes freeze: the sheet is never edited after, its checksum is re-verified at every subsequent act, and amendments ride the ledger, never the file. Nothing builds, reads, or runs until its sheet carries the stamp. Zero GPU at draft; zero decodes at the probe stage; the caches never leave the owner's machine; every run is owner-side.

## 3. Probes decide nothing

A diagnostic probe is a census tool and nothing else: it reads the pinned development caches and emits per-cell curves and censuses over the same monitor series the standing forms read. It carries no thresholds, no fires, no candidate numbers, no verdicts. Its receipts are read at the desk; the findings are presented; and the species sheets that might act on those findings open only at separate stamps — each grounded by the probe's receipts, or killed by them.

## 4. Species sheets — no improvement promised

Every species sheet pre-registers its own negative space: **no improvement is promised, before the bytes** — no lift, no coverage gain, no gap number. The test is allowed to answer no, and a sheet that answers no closes honestly. Closed sheets stay closed: their bytes never re-open, and their run-of-record receipts ride forward as motivation and as the embedded anchor table for the next sheet in the lineage.

## 5. The taxonomy, stated functionally

- **Contrast lenses.** A lens is selected for loop-versus-corpus contrast, never loop power alone: each document is normalized by its own off-lag behavior, so the corpus's baseline regularity cancels before any cross-document comparison happens.
- **Conjunction sheets.** Where two candidate signals are only jointly the deliverable, the conjunction is pre-registered as the species: neither lane is a deliverable alone, the primary number is the joint composite, and per-lane numbers ride as named decomposition consumers — never as separable instruments.
- **Conversion sheets.** The honest test on the held side: can a form convert a held-side miss into a detected one? No lift is promised; the sheet asks the coverage question directly, and the test is allowed to answer no.
- **The escalation order.** Coverage first, the cap check in the bar's own units, configuration after — the search order is fixed in advance, never improvised mid-development.

## 6. Development numbers never touch the record lane

The record lane's acceptance bar is computed on record-lane bytes at the record lane's own acts, never on development data. Development species carry their own level-sets, derived at the run, per leg — the sheets are gate-free by construction: no threshold travels from development into the record, and nothing a development sheet earns is wired without the record lane's own pre-registration passing first.

## 7. What this lane does not do

No threshold set, moved, or adjudicated on development data. No candidate promoted without the record lane's own stamped acts. No verdict rendered — findings are presented, and the owner's stamps decide. Nothing runs before its sheet carries the pinning stamp.
