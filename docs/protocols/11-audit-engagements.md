# Audit Engagements — the External-Review Boundary Protocol (Public Protocol Summary)

External analyses arrive at the owner's hand — audit documents from other models proposing joins, diagnoses, aggregations, and verdicts against the banked record. This document is the protocol record of how each engagement is received: critical, never blind; corrections kept, never flattered; premises tested, never assumed. Findings are presented; the owner's stamps decide.

## 1. The charge and the posture

Each audit arrives with the owner's charge: engage with the content — not blind acceptance, but never discard valid corrections. The act is a **boundary check**: verify the audit's arithmetic against the banked constants, test its factual premises against the caches and consoles of record, sort the viable from the broken, and present recommendations at the owner's stamps. Presented, never adjudicated — an audit's findings carry no program authority of their own.

## 2. The pattern — offline, pure, fail-loud

- **Pure arithmetic on banked constants.** Zero GPU, zero decodes, zero build hours. A standalone offline instrument — standard library only — verifies every printed number at the stated precision: each audit-printed value either reproduces exactly or is named as arithmetic drift.
- **Structural censuses, fail-loud.** Every premise an audit asserts about the caches of record is re-derived from the bytes by the instrument, with expected values locked before the run; a mismatch fails loud, is named, and is presented.
- **Guards on the instrument itself:** compile-clean; checksums derived programmatically at the run; the banned-token scan clean on every artifact; the banked composites untouched.

## 3. The no-shopping law — preliminary-read disclosure

Document-checking necessarily precedes pre-registration. The law: whatever the checking phase touched is disclosed in the pre-registration — structural reads only, no outcome reads — and the instrument re-derives every census fail-loud, so the preliminary read can be caught in error by its own expected values. Nothing is shopped on the banked record behind an undisclosed read.

## 4. The bar law — projections may be presented, verdicts may not

The separation bar's interpretation on a joined composite is the one question the boundary check holds open. A boundary check bounds, never reads, the bar: an audit may project, and a projection is presented as a projection — but an audit that renders a bar verdict on the joined composite has adjudicated a question that belongs to the join act at the owner's stamps, and the verdict is struck from the corrected directive. Doors are presented, never opened without stamps.

## 5. Provenance and population discipline

- **Receipt provenance.** Every number an audit presents as evidence is checked against the ledger of record: banked exact, attributed loosely, or the audit's own projection appearing nowhere in the record — each sort named. An audit's own paper projection re-labeled as a banked read is named as the slip it is.
- **Population coherence.** Every joined pairing is marked — same population, cross-unit, or cross-cohort. Cross-population pairings are derivable context, never reads. A population mix inside one claim is named as a mix.
- **Half-reads stay half-reads.** A sub-stratum read never carries its stratum's verdict; a token-level cohort count never reads as a document count; an unmeasured assumption is marked assumed, never measured.

## 6. Corrections kept, slips named — in both directions

The engagement keeps valid corrections: where an audit's reasoning repaired a statement the record had not covered, the correction is kept and the record's claim re-stated. And the discipline runs both directions — the record's own preliminary verdicts, when corrected by the ledger-anchored half of the check, are named too. The two-step discipline (a paper half, then a document-gated half) is designed so its own errors surface by construction. Slips are named as slips: garbled formulas, label errors, boundary verdicts — findings, never instrument trips.

## 7. What these engagements never do

No join performed, no bar read, no outcome read beyond the banked receipts. No composite reweighted, no feature dropped, no threshold set, moved, or adjudicated. Nothing armed, nothing wired. The audits sort viable from broken at the owner's stamps — and the stamps, only the stamps, decide.
